PRIVATE CLIENTS

Between homes, handled with discretion.

When you are between homes, downsizing or moving abroad, we collect what you want to keep, record it, and store it calmly until your next home is ready. You choose what moves and when, and pay only for the services you use.

The problem

A gap between two homes, and nowhere calm to put everything.

Removals firms quote to move a whole house in a day. Real life is rarely that tidy. Completion dates slip, rentals overlap, and belongings end up scattered between garages, lofts and borrowed spare rooms.

  • Removals quotes built for a whole house, not a transition
  • Belongings split between garages, lofts and storage units
  • No clear view of what went where
Our solution

A managed pause between one home and the next.

We handle the in-between. Collection and packing on your schedule, recorded storage for as long as the gap lasts, and delivery to your new address in the UK, or shipping overseas, when you are ready.

  • Careful collection and packing where you want it
  • Recorded storage for as long as the gap lasts
  • Scheduled redelivery in the UK, or shipping overseas
SCOPE OF WORK

What is included, and what you receive.

Everything below is available as part of this service, each element priced on its own line of your quote.

Pre-move collection

We collect what is going into storage ahead of moving day, so the move itself is lighter and calmer.

Careful packing on request

Fragile, valuable or awkward items can be packed by us with proper materials, priced per box.

Recorded storage between homes

Everything is photographed and logged, so you know exactly what is waiting for you.

Downsizing support

Moving somewhere smaller? We store what will not fit yet while you decide what to keep.

Scheduled redelivery

When your new home is ready, we deliver everything, or just the first rooms’ worth, on a date you choose.

Overseas relocation shipping

Moving abroad? We pack in Bath and arrange onward shipping from the same record.

HOW IT RUNS

Four steps from first call to final delivery.

The same calm sequence every time, with a record at each stage.

1

Talk through the move and timings

We agree what is moving, what is being stored and the dates that matter to you.

2

Collect and pack with care

Items are collected and, where you have asked, packed by us at your home.

3

Store with a full item record

Belongings are stored in Bath with a photographed record you can check at any time.

4

Deliver when your new home is ready

Everything is delivered to your new home on the date you choose.
