ABOUT MOVEASSIST

A managed service, built for belongings that matter.

MoveAssist was set up in Bath to do something couriers and removals firms do not: look after belongings over time, with a proper record, a clear price and one team accountable from start to finish.

Bath basedRegular London runs
OUR MISSION
To make moving and storing belongings feel calm, accountable and completely transparent, from the first collection to the final delivery.
Paul Harrison, Founder
OUR STORY

Not a courier. Not a removals firm.

Couriers move parcels from A to B. Removals firms move a whole house in a day. Neither is built for the months in between, when belongings need collecting, keeping track of and delivering somewhere new.

MoveAssist started with that gap. Boarding school families juggling terms and time zones, households caught between completion dates, and businesses with stock spilling into back rooms all needed the same thing: someone to take responsibility for their belongings, not just move them. So we built a managed service around one van, a recorded storage operation in Bath and a simple rule. Every item is labelled and photographed, every service is priced separately, and payment is agreed upfront so there are no surprises later.

OUR VALUES

Four principles behind every collection.

What guides the way we collect, store and deliver, whoever the client is.

Accountable by default

Every item we collect is labelled, photographed and logged before it leaves your hands. If something is in our care, we can tell you what it is, what condition it was in and exactly where it is. That record is the foundation of everything we do.

Transparent pricing

Collection, storage, packing, retrieval and delivery are priced separately, and you see each one on your quote. The adjustments that could apply on the day, such as stairs or a fixed time window, are listed before you pay. Nothing is added without you knowing.

Discretion as standard

We handle personal belongings, family possessions and business stock, often for clients who are not in the country. We treat every record, address and item with care, and share information only with the people you authorise.

Care over speed

We are not trying to be the fastest van on the road. We plan collections properly, pack to the standard each journey needs and confirm every hand-off, because getting it right first time matters more than getting it done quickly.

THE JOURNEY

How MoveAssist came to be.

From a simple idea to a managed service, built in Bath.

The starting point

A better way to move what matters

MoveAssist began with a simple observation: the hardest part of moving is rarely the journey itself. It is everything around it, from collections and storage to keeping track of what went where.

One van, one standard

A new Mercedes Vito, run from Bath

A 2026 Mercedes Vito XLWB in MoveAssist livery became the centre of the operation, based in Bath and running regularly to and from London.

StoreAssist takes shape

Storage managed, not simply rented

StoreAssist brought every stored item onto a single photographed record, so clients could see exactly what was held and ask for it back item by item.

Schools and guardians

Built around the school year

Boarding schools, guardians and international families became a core focus, with collections and returns planned around the school calendar.

What comes next

More storage sites, the same standard

Next, we plan to work alongside more storage partners while keeping the standard exactly the same: recorded, separately priced and accountable.
