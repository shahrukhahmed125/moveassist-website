STORAGE & LOGISTICS FIRMS

A managed service your customers can book through you.

Storage operators and logistics firms work with MoveAssist to offer collection, packing and delivery to their own customers. We operate alongside your facility, keep proper item records and give your team one named contact.

The problem

Your customers need more than a unit and a padlock.

Customers rent a unit and then face the hard part alone: hiring a van, packing, carrying, and coming back for one forgotten box. Some never book at all, because getting everything there feels like too much.

  • Customers asking for collection you cannot offer
  • Units left underused because moving in is hard
  • Transfers and redeliveries turned away
Our solution

A collection and delivery partner based on your site.

MoveAssist fills that gap. Customers book collection and packing alongside their unit, their belongings arrive recorded and organised, and transfers or redeliveries no longer get turned away.

  • Collection and packing booked alongside a unit
  • Item records your customers can trust
  • One named contact for your team
SCOPE OF WORK

What is included, and what you receive.

Everything below is available as part of this service, each element priced on its own line of your quote.

Collection service for your customers

We collect from customers’ homes or businesses and deliver into storage at your facility.

Packing on your customers’ behalf

Packing priced per box, so customers arrive with belongings that stack and store well.

Transfers between facilities

We move customers’ belongings between sites, or between unit sizes, with records intact.

Customer-facing item records

Customers receive a photographed list of everything that went into storage.

Referral arrangements

A simple, written arrangement for the customers you introduce to us.

One named operations contact

One person at MoveAssist for your team to call, whatever the job.

HOW IT RUNS

Four steps from first call to final delivery.

The same calm sequence every time, with a record at each stage.

1

Agree the service and referral model

We agree which services you want to offer and how referrals will work.

2

Introduce MoveAssist to your customers

Your team tells customers about collection, packing and delivery.

3

Collect, pack and record on their behalf

We carry out the work and keep item records for each customer.

4

Review volumes together each quarter

We review volumes and feedback together each quarter and adjust as needed.
