HOW IT WORKS

From doorstep to doorstep, in five recorded steps.

01

Collected from your door

We arrive in the agreed window at your home, boarding house or business, in the signwritten MoveAssist van.

02

Labelled and photographed

Every item gets its own tag and photograph before it leaves, building a record you can check at any time.

03

Stored in a recorded bay

Belongings are stored securely in Bath, each logged against its location, so nothing is ever guesswork.

04

Packed for the journey

When it is time to move on, we pack to the standard the journey needs, from a short hop to an overseas shipment.

05

Delivered where it is needed

We deliver to your door, a boarding house for the new term, a London address, or onward anywhere in the world.

STOREASSIST

The record behind every move.

StoreAssist is the record behind every MoveAssist service. You always know what we hold, what it looks like and exactly where it is.

  • Only the space you need

    We size storage around your belongings, so you never pay for empty floor space.

  • One item, or everything

    Ask for a single box, the bike or everything at once. Each retrieval is priced on its own.

  • Photographed on arrival

    Every item is photographed when it arrives, so its condition is on record from day one.

How StoreAssist works

Item-level records

A reference, a photograph and a location for every single item.

Right-sized storage

Space planned around what you own, not the biggest unit available.

Separately priced

Collection, storage, packing, retrieval and delivery, each itemised.

Planned around term

Collections and returns that follow the school calendar.

One point of contact

One team that knows your record, from first call to final delivery.
