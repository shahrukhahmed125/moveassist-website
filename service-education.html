SCHOOLS, GUARDIANS & FAMILIES

A student’s belongings, looked after from first term to last.

We collect from boarding houses at the end of term, store everything on a photographed record in Bath, and have it back in a student’s room for the first day of the next. Parents, guardians and schools all deal with one team and one price list.

The problem

Four suppliers, three time zones and a boarding house deadline.

End of term arrives at once for a whole house. Parents are often overseas, guardians are juggling several students, and belongings end up spread across friends’ garages, rented units and couriers that each need booking separately.

  • End-of-term collections that clash across a whole house
  • Storage rebooked every summer instead of planned once
  • No single record of what a student owns, or where it is
Our solution

One managed record for a student’s entire UK education.

MoveAssist plans collections around the school calendar and keeps a single record of what each student owns. Whether it is a summer of storage, an exeat or the final move home, the belongings and the paperwork stay in one place.

  • Collections scheduled around the school calendar
  • Every item labelled, photographed and stored
  • Returned for the new term, or shipped home when they leave
SCOPE OF WORK

What is included, and what you receive.

Everything below is available as part of this service, each element priced on its own line of your quote.

Boarding house collections

We agree collection windows with the house team, so trunks, cases and boxes leave on the day that suits the school.

Term-time & summer storage

Belongings are stored in Bath for as long as needed, from a single half-term to a long summer, priced per item, per week.

Photographed item records

Every trunk, case and box is labelled and photographed at collection, so parents can see exactly what is held.

Exeat & half-term moves

Short breaks can be covered too, with items brought back in good time for the student’s return.

Return to school or new accommodation

At the start of term we deliver to the boarding house, a new house or university accommodation, ready for move-in day.

Shipping home at the end of school

When a student leaves, we pack and ship their belongings home, in the UK or overseas, from the same record.

HOW IT RUNS

Four steps from first call to final delivery.

The same calm sequence every time, with a record at each stage.

1

Register with your school or guardian

Tell us the student, the school, the term dates and who is authorised to arrange collections.

2

Collection at the end of term

We collect from the boarding house in the agreed window and label every item before it leaves.

3

Recorded and stored in Bath

Items are photographed, logged and stored, and the record is shared with parents and guardians.

4

Returned for the new term

Everything comes back for the first day of term, or goes onwards to wherever the student is heading next.
